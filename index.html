Get to know me!

I am Dmytro Pahuba, based in Dortmund. Since January 2022 I have been a working student at the RIF Institut für Forschung und Transfer, on two publicly funded digital forestry projects. My side of them is the software a forester actually holds: cross-platform applications in Flutter that put a soil model on a map, turn machines and sensors into things you can talk to, and keep working when the phone drops off the network in a wood. The two bots on this page came from somewhere else entirely, one from a flood and one from my own trading, and both are backend work.

In parallel I am doing a B.Sc. in Applied Computer Science at TU Dortmund. The code that went out to real users is Dart, Python and JavaScript. The degree added the rest: a term long team project in Kotlin, TypeScript and Sass in the web modules, Java and C further back, and a lot of Python across data warehousing, compiler construction and machine learning.
